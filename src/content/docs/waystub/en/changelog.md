## v1.0 *November 9, 2026*

### First Release

- Import train and plane tickets from a screenshot: train or flight number, stations, date and seat are read for you, and outbound and return tickets become one journey.
- Also reads e-invoices and itineraries (PDF / OFD), Wallet boarding passes, and ticket texts or emails, or add a trip by hand.
- Photos and videos from the days of each trip are gathered into the journey, with Live Photos and videos playing right in the app.
- Footprint map: cities visited, distance traveled and your most frequent routes, with round trips merged once you set your home city.
- Year report and On This Day, with widgets and notifications.
- Share cards and ticket papers in many styles, optimized for iPhone Duo.
