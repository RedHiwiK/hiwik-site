## v1.0.2 *November 13, 2026*

### New Features

- Import tickets before you go: upcoming trips show an "Upcoming" tag and a countdown, and you can find the photos when you're back.
- For past trips without photos, find the photos from those days right from the journey page.
- Footprint now lists the countries and regions you've visited.

### Improvements and Fixes

- Bug fixes.

## v1.0.1 *November 12, 2026*

### Improvements and Fixes

- When one ticket of a round trip was imported before, the other one can be merged into that journey.
- Importing the same ticket again from another screenshot, message or text is now recognized, with a shortcut to the existing journey.
- More accurate recognition of PDF invoices and flight itineraries, and rail invoices no longer show the wrong departure station.
- Fixed clipped and overlapping text on smaller iPhones and in English.
- Ticket styles now show their own descriptions.

## v1.0 *November 9, 2026*

### First Release

- Import train and plane tickets from a screenshot: train or flight number, stations, date and seat are read for you, and outbound and return tickets become one journey.
- Also reads e-invoices and itineraries (PDF / OFD), Wallet boarding passes, and ticket texts or emails, or add a trip by hand.
- Photos and videos from the days of each trip are gathered into the journey, with Live Photos and videos playing right in the app.
- Footprint map: cities visited, distance traveled and your most frequent routes, with round trips merged once you set your home city.
- Year report and On This Day, with widgets and notifications.
- Share cards and ticket papers in many styles, optimized for iPhone Duo.
