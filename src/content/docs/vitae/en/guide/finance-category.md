Vitae comes with built-in categories like Food, Transport, and Shopping, each with finer sub-categories. You can adjust their <strong>order</strong> and hide the ones <strong>you don't use</strong> — once arranged, categories appear in your order and preferences when logging, with frequently used ones up front so picking is faster.

## Steps

1. <strong>Open Category Management</strong>. Path: <strong>Settings → Finance → Category Management</strong>, where all top-level categories are listed (Food, Transport, Shopping…).
2. <strong>Reorder top-level categories</strong>. Tap the <strong>reorder icon ()</strong> in the top-right to enter reorder mode, then press and drag categories up or down to move frequently used ones to the front.
3. <strong>Manage sub-categories</strong>. Tap into any top-level category (e.g. "Food") to see its sub-categories (Meals, Takeout, Drinks…): each has a toggle controlling whether it shows when logging, and you can also tap in the top-right to reorder.
4. <strong>Whole-category visibility toggle</strong>. The <strong>"Show when logging"</strong> switch at the top of a category's detail page controls whether the entire top-level category appears when logging.

## Add Your Own Categories

Built-in categories not enough? At the bottom of a category's detail page, tap <strong>"Add Sub-category"</strong>, then <strong>pick an icon and give it a name</strong> (e.g. add "Dining" under "Food"). After saving, it appears among your categories when logging, just like the built-in ones.

1. <strong>Open a top-level category</strong>. In Category Management, tap into a category like "Food" to see its existing sub-category list.
2. <strong>Tap "Add Sub-category" at the bottom</strong>. The button is at the very bottom of the sub-category list.
3. <strong>Pick an icon + name it</strong>. Choose an icon from the icon grid and enter a category name.
4. <strong>Save</strong>. The new category is added to this top-level category right away, ready to pick when logging.

## Once Arranged: Smoother Logging

Once category order and visibility are set, when you go back to <strong>manual logging</strong>, the category picker shows exactly the way you defined it — <strong>frequently used ones up front, hidden ones gone</strong>. No more scrolling through a long list every time; logging an entry is faster.

## FAQ

### How do I move a category to the very front?

In <strong>Settings → Finance → Category Management</strong>, tap the reorder icon () in the top-right, then press and drag that category to the top. Sub-categories work the same way on their category's detail page.

### Some categories I never use — can I remove them?

Yes. Tap into that category and turn off the toggles for sub-categories you don't use; or simply turn off "Show when logging" at the top, and the whole category won't appear when logging. Turn it back on anytime you need it.

### Will reordering or hiding affect entries I've already logged?

No. These settings only affect the <strong>display order and visibility</strong> of categories when logging; your existing records are not affected in any way.

### Built-in categories aren't enough — can I add my own?

Yes. Tap into the relevant top-level category, tap <strong>"Add Sub-category"</strong> at the bottom of the sub-category list, then pick an icon, name it, and save. The new category will appear in your choices when logging.
