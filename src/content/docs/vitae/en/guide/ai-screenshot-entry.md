No more typing entries one by one. Hand a bill screenshot from Alipay, WeChat, shopping apps, food-delivery apps, or your bank to the AI, and it automatically recognizes every transaction in the image—filling in the amount, time, and category for you, generating a "to confirm" list. Just review it and batch-save.

## Steps

1. <strong>Open "Finance → Ledger Entry" and go to AI Receipt Capture.</strong>
2. <strong>Pick a bill screenshot</strong>. Alipay / WeChat, shopping apps, food-delivery apps, bank statements, and payment-success pages all work—the clearer the image and the more readable the text, the better.
3. <strong>Let the AI recognize it</strong>. It extracts the amount and time from each transaction in the image and suggests a category—a single bill list can often surface several entries at once.
4. <strong>Review one by one, batch confirm</strong>. Results appear as a list where you can change the category, edit the amount, and choose an account for each entry, or delete a misrecognized one entirely; save them all at once after reviewing.
5. <strong>Done</strong>. The saved records all appear in your ledger entry list.

## Why "Batch Confirm"

Records recognized by the AI <strong>are not booked directly</strong>; they first enter a to-confirm list for you to review before saving. There are a few reasons for this design:

- <strong>One image, many entries.</strong> Bill-list screenshots often contain several transactions, and the AI recognizes them all at once—no need to screenshot and record each one separately.
- <strong>Fast, but not blind.</strong> Auto-fill saves you from typing, but amounts and categories occasionally come out wrong—confirming before booking lets you enjoy the speed of automation without fear of mis-recording.
- <strong>Easy to edit and delete.</strong> One tap switches the category, amounts can be corrected by hand, and a clearly wrong entry can be deleted on its own without affecting the rest.
- <strong>Originals aren't kept.</strong> The screenshot is used only for this one recognition and isn't stored afterward; your ledger data always stays in your own iCloud.

## FAQ

### What if the AI gets the amount or category wrong?

Before saving, every entry in the to-confirm list can be edited or deleted; even after saving, you can change it anytime in the ledger entry list.

### Which screenshots are supported?

Bill / statement screenshots from Alipay, WeChat, shopping apps, food-delivery apps, and various bank apps, as well as payment-success pages. The clearer the image and the more complete the text, the more accurate the recognition.

### How many entries can I capture at once?

It depends on the screenshot content. A single payment page is one entry; a bill-list screenshot can recognize multiple entries at once and batch-save them.

### Will my screenshots be uploaded?

Images are used only when you actively trigger recognition this time; the original images are not kept and are not uploaded to any third-party server.
