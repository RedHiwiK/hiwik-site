Thought of something on the fly? No need to fill out a form field by field. In <strong>Todo</strong>, <strong>just say a sentence with your voice</strong>, like "Meeting tomorrow morning at 10:00," and the AI automatically turns it into a todo, even recognizing the <strong>reminder time</strong> and <strong>category</strong> for you.

## How to use it

1. <strong>Open the Todo module</strong> and start <strong>voice input</strong> (tap the microphone button).
2. <strong>Say your todo.</strong> When the screen shows "Listening…", speak naturally, like "Meeting tomorrow morning at 10:00," and the text will be transcribed in real time below.
3. <strong>Tap "Done".</strong> The AI organizes the sentence into a todo, automatically filling in the title, <strong>reminder time</strong>, and <strong>category</strong>.
4. <strong>Go back to the list to check.</strong> The new todo appears under "To do" on the matching date, and it will remind you at the recognized time.

## What the AI can recognize

- <strong>Todo content</strong>: turns what you say into a concise todo title.
- <strong>Time and reminder</strong>: recognizes phrases like "tomorrow morning at 10:00" and automatically sets the date and reminder.
- <strong>Category</strong>: judges which category it belongs to based on the content (such as "Life") and tags it automatically.

## FAQ

### What if it recognizes something wrong?

Just tap the todo to edit it—the title, time, reminder, and category can all be adjusted manually. AI recognition only saves you from typing; your edited version is what counts in the end.

### Can I use it without saying a time?

Yes. If you only mention the task without a time, it creates a todo with no reminder; if you want to add a reminder later, just open it and fill in the time.

### Can I change the category if it's wrong?

Yes. Just open the todo and switch to a different category. For adding or removing categories themselves, see [Category Management and Custom Categories](/en/vitae/guide/todo-category/).
