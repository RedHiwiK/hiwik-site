Daily logging starts from "Finance → Ledger." Tap the plus button in the bottom-right corner to add a new income or expense.

<video src="/vitae/videos/finance-quick-entry-demo.mp4" controls playsinline preload="metadata" poster="/vitae/images/screenshots/tutorials/finance-quick-entry-filled.png"></video>

## Steps

1. <strong>Open the "Finance" module</strong> and switch to the "Ledger" tab.
2. <strong>Tap the "+" in the bottom-right corner</strong> to open the new entry page.
3. <strong>Choose Income or Expense</strong>. Expense fits spending like shopping, dining, and transport; Income fits earnings like salary, bonuses, and refunds.
4. <strong>Pick a category and enter the amount</strong>. Just type the number for the amount—no plus or minus sign needed.
5. <strong>Tap "Done" to confirm</strong>, and the record will appear in the ledger list.

## Continuous Logging: Use "Log Again"

When you want to log several entries in one go, there's no need to return to the list and re-enter for each one. After saving the current entry, tap <strong>"Log Again"</strong> on the new entry page to continue with the next one—logging the day's transactions back to back feels much smoother.

## FAQ

### What if I logged it wrong?

Tap the record in the ledger list to edit its amount, category, time, account, and notes, or to delete it.

### Do I have to choose an account?

Not necessarily. Accounts are used to sync with asset balances. If you just want to log a transaction first, you can leave the account unselected.

### Should I enter expense amounts as negative numbers?

No. Always enter amounts as positive numbers; whether it's an Expense or Income is decided by the toggle at the top.
