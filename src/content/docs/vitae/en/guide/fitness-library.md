Vitae comes with a built-in <strong>exercise library</strong> covering Chest, Back, Shoulder, Arms, Legs, Core, and Cardio, with every exercise tagged by <strong>muscle group</strong> and <strong>tracking type</strong>. You can quickly filter by muscle group and add exercises to your workout plan; and if it's not in the library, you can <strong>create your own</strong>.

## 1. Browse & Filter by Muscle Group

When you build a plan and tap "+" to add an exercise in <strong>Health → Exercise</strong>, you enter the exercise library. The row of tabs at the top—<strong>All / Chest / Back / Shoulder / Arms / Legs / Core / Cardio</strong>—lets you tap to view only that muscle group's exercises, and you can also search exercise names directly with the search box above.

- <strong>Muscle group</strong>: the gray tag at the bottom-left of each exercise (Chest, Back, Shoulder…), so you can pick by target muscle.
- <strong>Tracking type</strong>: the blue tag on the right shows how the exercise is logged—<strong>weight × reps</strong> (barbell, dumbbell types), <strong>reps</strong> (crunches, ab wheel), <strong>time</strong> (plank, battle ropes), <strong>distance × time</strong> (cycling).
- <strong>Add to plan</strong>: tap the blue <strong>"+"</strong> on the right of an exercise to add it to your current workout plan.

## 2. Create a Custom Exercise

Can't find the exercise you want in the library? Tap the <strong>"+" in the top-right corner</strong> of the exercise library to create a new one: enter the exercise name, choose the muscle group, choose the tracking type, and once saved it works just like a built-in exercise and can be added to any plan.

All custom exercises you create can be viewed and deleted in <strong>Settings → Health → Custom Exercise Management</strong>.

## 3. Fitness-Related Settings

In <strong>Settings → Health</strong>, there are a few switches related to fitness:

- <strong>Exercise Illustration Gender</strong>: switch between male / female exercise illustrations, whichever you prefer.
- <strong>Rest-End Reminder</strong>: whether to send a notification when the between-set rest countdown ends.
- <strong>Custom Exercise Management</strong>: manage the custom exercises you've created.

## FAQ

### Can I change the tracking type if I picked the wrong one?

Yes. Go to <strong>Settings → Health → Custom Exercise Management</strong>, find that exercise, and edit it again (the tracking type of built-in exercises is fixed).

### Can I switch the illustrations to the female version?

Yes. Go to <strong>Settings → Health → Exercise Illustration Gender</strong> and switch to "Female," and all exercise illustrations will change accordingly.

### Can custom exercises be added to plans?

Yes. Once created, it appears in the exercise library just like built-in exercises—when building a plan, tap "+" to add it. For how to build a plan, see [Build a Workout Plan + Start Training](/en/vitae/guide/fitness-plan/).
