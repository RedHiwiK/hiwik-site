Vitae brings Finance, Health, Fitness, Todo, and Self Know together in one app. You can <strong>switch</strong> between them anytime, <strong>hide</strong> the modules you don't use, or adjust their <strong>display order</strong>—keeping only what you actually need.

## 1. Switching between modules

There are two ways to open the "Modules" drawer: <strong>swipe right from the left edge</strong> of the screen, or tap the <strong>icon in the top-left corner</strong> of the home page. Once the drawer slides out, tap any module icon—<strong>Finance / Health / Todo / Self Know</strong>—to switch to it, and the home page's stats and heatmap will update to that module's content.

## 2. Hiding and reordering modules

Path: <strong>Settings → Module Management</strong>. Here you decide which modules appear and in what order.

- <strong>Hide a module</strong>: turn off the toggle on the right of a module, and it disappears from "Modules" on the home page.
- <strong>Reorder</strong>: press and hold the drag handle (≡) on the right of a module and drag it up or down to change its order on the home page.
- <strong>Two limits</strong>: at least one module must stay enabled; "Settings" is always kept and can't be turned off (its icon shows a small lock).

## FAQ

### There are too many features—what if I don't use some of them?

Go to <strong>Settings → Module Management</strong> and turn off the toggles for modules you don't use. They'll be hidden from the home page, making the interface much cleaner right away. For example, if you only want to track expenses, you can keep just "Finance" and turn off Health, Todo, and Self Know.

### Can I bring back a hidden module later?

Yes. Go back to <strong>Settings → Module Management</strong> and turn its toggle back on—it'll return with all its data intact and nothing lost.

### Can I turn off all the modules?

No, at least one module must stay enabled. Also, "Settings" always stays available so you can come back and adjust things anytime.

### How do I put the modules I use most at the top?

In <strong>Settings → Module Management</strong>, press and hold the drag handle (≡) on the right of a module and drag it up or down. Drag your most-used ones to the top so they're a single tap away when switching.
