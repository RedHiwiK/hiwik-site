## v1.5.0 *October 20, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>New module: Belongings</strong>: add items with a photo or an order screenshot, import them from your ledger, or find a product image by name. Track price, location and tags. Open it from the module switcher.
- <strong>Cost per use</strong>: turn on usage tracking for things you rarely use, like a camera or a tent. Log each use to see the cost per use, and a heatmap shows what is gathering dust.
- <strong>Organize and reminders</strong>: add items to the organize box and go through them one by one. Get reminded before warranties, expiry dates and maintenance are due, and silence the ones that have already lapsed.
- <strong>30 new exercises</strong>: Smith machine, pec deck, kettlebell swings, farmer's carry and more. Over 110 built-in exercises, each illustrated in male and female versions.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Vitae now takes up less iCloud space and syncs faster.

## v1.4.9 *October 8, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>Data export</strong>: export your finance, health and other data as a JSON file from Settings → General, ready for an AI assistant to analyze.

### Improvements & Fixes

- In regions that use a comma as the decimal separator, balances, limits, exchange rates and nutrition values can now be entered normally. Balances also accept negative numbers.
- Screenshot recognition now records times in your phone's current time zone, so they're no longer off by hours when you're abroad.
- Foods added from photos now go straight into the meal you picked.

## v1.4.8 *September 26, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>More currencies</strong>: added EUR, JPY, GBP, KRW, SGD, AUD and TWD — search to find a currency quickly.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Improved number input, date picking and various interaction details.

## v1.4.7 *September 15, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>Faster refund entries</strong>: record a refund directly from an expense. The amount, account and note are prefilled and remain editable.
- <strong>Clearer day reminders</strong>: notifications now include the day's name and remaining time, so each reminder is clear at a glance.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Improved tab bar and quick-entry behavior on earlier iOS versions for a more reliable experience.

## v1.4.6 *August 28, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>Multi-currency tracking</strong>: each transaction takes the currency of its account, with the exchange rate locked in at the moment you record it — or overridden with the rate the transaction actually used. Every entry displays in its own currency, so spending abroad no longer means converting by hand.
- <strong>Transfers for credit cards and loans</strong>: credit accounts can now take part in transfers — money out is recorded as a cash advance, money in as a repayment. Overpayments are supported too (a negative balance owed means credit on the account).
- <strong>Keep only the modules you use</strong>: pick which modules to turn on the first time you launch the app and switch off the rest; you can change your mind any time under Settings → Modules. The onboarding tour has been rebuilt as well.
- <strong>Faster food picking</strong>: the food selector is now split into All / Favorites / Custom, with your frequent items sorted by how often you log them.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Fixed the whole page being pushed off-screen when a text field takes focus on iOS 26; field heights are now locked app-wide, so there's no more "jump" the first time you tap in.
- Users who have already granted access no longer see a flash of the HealthKit permission screen when opening the Health overview.
- The account picker in the ledger now shows your custom account icons.
- Deleting the last transaction of a day in the balance timeline now takes you back automatically instead of leaving you on an empty page.
- Exchange rates now come from our own service for more reliable updates.

## v1.4.5 *August 25, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>One-tap install for the screenshot shortcut</strong>: install the receipt-scanning shortcut straight from the guide page and choose from four triggers — automatically after a screenshot, the share sheet, a widget or Siri — with an illustrated walkthrough included.
- <strong>Icons for buy-now-pay-later brands</strong>: a new consumer-credit group brings icons for Huabei, Jiebei, JD Baitiao, WeiLiDai, Meituan Monthly Pay and Douyin Monthly Pay, so credit accounts no longer have to settle for a generic icon.
- <strong>Transfer fees</strong>: account transfers can now record a separate fee, with the sending account settled at the amount actually debited.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Fixed the Dynamic Island freezing on a stale workout state and showing the wrong exercise when you jump around your routine.
- Tapping a meal in the diet widget now goes straight to its logging page, without flashing the finance page and health permission screen first.

## v1.4.4 *August 7, 2026*

### Improvements & Fixes

- Rapid taps on a workout set card are no longer misread as completing the set, and the input field stays clear of the keyboard.
- Widgets and diet reminders now refresh against your new targets as soon as you change them.
- Tapping a diet reminder opens the logging form for that specific meal; opening the diet page from a widget or notification no longer flashes a loading placeholder.
- The tab bar no longer appears a beat late when returning from the diet logging page.

## v1.4.3 *August 2, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>Diet reminders that pick their moment</strong>: reminders follow the meal times you set, and nutrition gaps are only announced while there's still time to eat — once you've hit your targets for the day, they stay quiet.
- <strong>Diet widgets</strong>: four new widgets — quick log, today's intake, the three macros, and a diet streak — each tapping through to the right page. The to-do widget has been rebuilt to the new spec as well.
- <strong>Brand icons for international institutions</strong>: bank, brokerage and card-network icons now cover many more institutions outside China, and investment accounts can pick from the full set.
- <strong>Drag to reorder accounts</strong>: long-press and drag accounts within a group to put the ones you use most at the top.

### Improvements & Fixes

- The workout heatmap no longer freezes on cold-start data, and days with records are no longer drawn as empty cells.
- Fixed the set-card expand animation wrapping the rest-page transition; the tab bar no longer appears a beat late when returning from the rest page.

## v1.4.2 *July 30, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>AI health analysis</strong>: the Self-Knowledge module now reads your training and body data together and gives actionable training-load advice grounded in exercise science, rather than just listing numbers.
- <strong>Exercise progress details</strong>: weight, sets and volume trends for a single exercise now get their own page, reachable directly from a notification.
- <strong>Days joins the onboarding tour</strong>: the first-launch walkthrough now introduces the Days module too.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Fixed lunar / repeat / reminder settings being dropped on the edit page when importing days from a screenshot.
- Editing an asset account no longer overwrites the latest balance with a stale form value.
- Fixed AI recognition filing income entries under an expense category.
- Workout set cards now expand with an animation and the complete button has a brief tap guard; the default rest between sets is a uniform 2 minutes instead of varying by muscle group.
- Fixed the tab bar sitting flush against the bottom edge of the screen.

## v1.4.0 *July 26, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>All-new Days module</strong>: track anniversaries, birthdays and milestones, with both countdowns and days-since at a glance. Supports lunar / solar dates, repeats, pinning, category management and search, plus list and calendar views.
- <strong>Reminder management hub</strong>: new days default to a 9:00 AM reminder on the day itself, and the reminders page groups everything into "On" and "Off" — tap any row to open an editor and adjust how many days ahead and at what time.
- <strong>Bulk import from screenshots</strong>: import multiple days at once from list screenshots of Days Matter and similar apps, then batch-select, recategorize or delete the results — no more typing them in one by one.
- <strong>Days widgets</strong>: three new widget styles — Classic, List and Grid. Long-press to configure a specific day or category, and tap to jump straight to that day's details.
- <strong>Workout progress on your Lock Screen</strong>: once a session starts, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show your current exercise, set progress and workout timer in real time, switching to a countdown between sets — no need to reopen the app.
- <strong>Transfers between accounts</strong>: move money directly between any two accounts, with balances and transfer records updated on both sides, plus a dedicated transfer category and icon.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Polished the screenshot recognition flow: the original image preview is gone after picking a photo, recognition shows a centered loading state, the result action bar stays visible, and a failed scan lets you swap in another image right away.
- Fixed the workout Lock Screen card rendering only its icon on real devices, with text and timer missing; the workout icon is now a more fitting dumbbell.
- Fixed Days widgets opening the app without navigating to the Days module; widget gallery previews now use sample data instead of showing an empty state.
- Several fixes across the ledger and search pages: no more flicker when opening the ledger for the first time, no more jump on the search page's first render, and the "More filters" page no longer has content hidden behind the toolbar.
- Onboarding is no longer covered by the "Continue" button on small screens — you can scroll through the content normally.

## v1.3.0 *July 7, 2026*

### What's New

- <strong>Credit card / loan limit tracking</strong>: accounts now use a "balance owed + total limit" model, so you can see at a glance how much you've spent and how much room you have left — recording a charge past the limit now warns you before it happens.
- <strong>Pay off credit cards / loans directly</strong>: pick a source account on the account detail page to complete a repayment transfer in one go; all views now consistently show the amount owed, and overpayment is validated.
- <strong>Search across all bills</strong>: filter by category, date range and amount range, with flexible sorting — finding a past transaction is much faster.
- <strong>Repayment reminders</strong>: ledger accounts can now have a due date / statement date with a custom reminder and a countdown badge.
- <strong>What's New popup</strong>: after updating to a new version, you'll see a concise "What's New" popup — no need to check the changelog page yourself.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Loan repayments no longer hardcode the "mortgage" subcategory — they're now categorized by the loan's actual type.
- Fixed alignment of the source-account card on the repayment page, and a "negative zero" display bug for the remaining balance.
- Removed the balance-history chart from credit card / loan detail pages; "Transaction history" is renamed to "Statement records" for a more focused page.
- The ledger page now defaults to showing "All accounts" as its title; the search page's filter arrow / clear button now use colored icons, and the active sort option is highlighted more clearly.

## v1.2.0 *June 27, 2026*
