## FAQ

### Is my data safe?

Yes. Vitae never stores your business data on our servers. All business data — ledger, assets, health, fitness, todos, reflections — is stored via SwiftData on your device and in the private iCloud database under your Apple ID. Even if the App were discontinued, your data would remain intact.

### How do I sync across devices?

Vitae syncs via Apple CloudKit. Make sure: (1) all your devices are signed in to the same Apple ID; (2) Vitae is enabled in System Settings → Apple ID → iCloud; (3) iCloud Sync is on inside the App's Settings. First-time sync may take 1–2 minutes.

### What if AI receipt capture gets things wrong?

Tap 'Scan Receipt' while adding an entry and AI fills in amount, time and category for you — <strong>please double-check the key fields before saving</strong>. The original image is not persisted. If the recognition is clearly off, attach the screenshot via Settings → App Feedback and we'll keep improving it.

### What data does AI Reflections use?

The 'Self Know' module can generate a <strong>weekly or monthly review</strong> in one tap. AI mines the period for highlights, patterns and questions worth asking. Explicit consent is required on first use; there is a daily call cap. The process <strong>does not send raw images and does not send sensitive raw data like health or location</strong> — only brief text from the modules you opted in.

### What's built into the diet / fitness modules?

The diet module ships with <strong>45</strong> common foods (covering staples, meat & dairy, seafood, vegetables, fruit and legumes) — each with nutrition values and an icon, ready to log in one tap; you can also create custom foods. The fitness module ships with <strong>83</strong> standard exercises (chest 11 / back 12 / shoulder 10 / arms 14 / legs 15 / core 10 / cardio 12) across four tracking types (weight+reps / reps / duration / distance). You can build your own training plans from these exercises.

### Where do the health / diet ring data come from?

The activity ring uses Move / Exercise / Stand from Apple Health; the diet ring uses protein / carbs / fat macros recorded in the diet module. You can fine-tune HealthKit access in Health App → Data Access & Devices → Vitae.

### What do Shortcuts / Lock Screen widgets support?

Two App Intents are built in: <strong>'Quick Add Todo'</strong> (with voice + natural-language) and <strong>'Scan Receipt'</strong> (recognize and save an entry without opening the app). The Lock Screen / Home widget offers one-tap 'Add Todo'. You can trigger them via Shortcuts, Siri, Control Center, etc.

### Which devices and OS versions are supported?

Vitae currently supports iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later. Devices signed in to the same Apple ID share the same iCloud data.

### Can I export my data?

Yes. Members can go to Settings → General → Export Data, choose modules (Finance, Health, To-Do, Days, Self-Knowing) and a time range, and export a single .json file to share with an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude, or save to Files. Exported files can't currently be imported back.

### How do I delete all my data?

Uninstalling Vitae removes all local data. To also clear iCloud data, go to Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on iPhone, find Vitae and delete its data.

### How do I report an issue?

Settings → App Feedback inside the app gives you multiple channels, or email us directly via the address below.
