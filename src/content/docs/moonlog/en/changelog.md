## v1.4.0 *September 10, 2026*

### What's New

- Futures support: 30 major CME contracts across index, metals, energy, agriculture and rates, including micros.
- Short positions: plans can go long or short, with stops, targets, P&L and health all direction-aware.
- R-multiple scale: profit distribution switches to R units when futures are present, so leverage no longer squeezes every trade into the middle buckets.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Warns when an entered price is far off the usual range, so a mistyped magnitude no longer skews your stats.
- AI review now understands shorts and contract multipliers, and no longer flags a short's stop-loss as misconfigured.
- Futures show settlement price and crypto shows last price, instead of all being labelled closing price.
- Data export now includes market, position direction and contract multiplier, so exports can be read back in full.
