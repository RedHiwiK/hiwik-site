- Pick your usual currency in Cottage Settings › Personalization › Currency, and your expense book will show the matching symbol

- Bug fixes and tweaks to make capturing every cute moment smoother

### Improvements

- Bug fixes and tweaks to make capturing every cute moment smoother

## v2.4.5 *September 2026*

### A new Clothing category for supplies

- Sweaters, collars and leashes now have their own Clothing category, so they're easy to find

### Improvements

- Entry details no longer show the time twice, for a cleaner look
- Bug fixes and tweaks to make capturing every cute moment smoother

## v2.4.4 *September 2026*

### Highlights, redesigned

- A dedicated keepsake detail page, with lit and unlit highlights browsed separately
- Hand-drawn covers even without photos, or pick a photo and adjust the framing yourself
- Turn preset highlights on or off, so types you don't track no longer count toward progress

### A fresh new Home

- Newly drawn Home backgrounds: eight hand-drawn scenes to match every season

### Improvements

- Reorder diary types freely, and filter by custom types too
- Clearer pet selection for multi-pet reminders; highlight cards adapt to different screen sizes
- Fixed a number of small UI issues

## v2.4.3 *September 2026*

### More precise records

- Weight supports up to two decimal places, entered in kg or jin
- Set one weight display unit for everything in Personalization

### Improvements

- Single-pet households no longer see a redundant "All" option
- Fixed date and entry-type filters sometimes missing older entries
- Refreshed illustrations for highlight cards, the entry limit notice and the Pro page

## v2.4.1 *September 2026*

### More reliable iCloud sync

- When switching devices or signing in again, pets and entries come back faster and more completely
- Home name and background (including your own photos) sync via iCloud, so a new device no longer falls back to the default look
- Daily reminder time, notification switches and other preferences stay consistent across devices

### Improvements

- Better keyboard dismissal and input row layout in the supplies form

## v2.4.0 *August 2026*

### Pet supplies management

- Keep track of pet food, medicine, cleaning products, toys and more in one place
- Record quantity, location, photos, owner pet and expiry date
- Get reminded before items expire, so nothing is forgotten or wasted
- Sort by date added, expiry date or quantity

### Improvements

- Rebuilt Home and pet management with clearer shortcuts
- Updated hand-drawn illustrations and empty states throughout
- Fixed issues with the search keyboard, page bounce and form layout

## v2.3.0 *August 2026*

### A new look for health records

- Weight, vaccines, deworming and vet visits each have their own hand-drawn watercolor icon, easy to tell apart in lists and filters

### Clearer expense charts

- New colors: one color per expense category, no more crowded charts
- Weight gains and losses use different colors, so you can tell at a glance

### Polish

- Better framing for Home backgrounds, with no more distorted crops
- Empty-state illustrations for diary and reminders are now the same size, so switching no longer jumps
- The timeline refreshes immediately after a pet is deleted, with no leftover blank cards

## v2.2.1 *August 2026*

- The user group now has both Xiaohongshu QR code and Douyin entry points, making it easier to join

## v2.2.0 *August 2026*

### Create your own share images

- Five curated layouts; 2 to 9 photos arranged automatically into a collage
- Ten paper backgrounds, text bubbles and pet stickers to mix and match; every sticker can be dragged, scaled and rotated
- Switch between 3:4 for posts and 9:16 for stories, ready to export

### New features

- Nine new illustration sets for dogs, rabbits, hamsters, birds and more, switchable anytime
- Zoom and frame your Home background to keep your favorite part in view
- Gentle reminders for 100 days home, birthdays and homecoming anniversaries
- The starting weight you enter when adding a pet now goes into the weight chart; if it's been a while, you'll be reminded it's time to weigh in

### Improvements

- Subtle toasts and haptic feedback for saving and deleting
- Faster saving for multi-photo entries, smoother timeline scrolling and more responsive sticker dragging
- Added VoiceOver labels and currency localization
